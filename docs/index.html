Mechanism Architecture
  • Blind Purchase Recourse: Mandatory seller staking (0.01+ ETH) secures trust prior to payload inspection.
  • Proof-of-Reveal: Enforces keccak256(plaintext) == contentCommitment on-chain at reveal time.
  • Dispute Deterrent: Buyer must post a 0.001 ETH dispute bond, forfeited to the seller if the claim is baseless.
  • Stake Slashing: Losing arbitration slashes 0.005 ETH from the seller stake to compensate the buyer.
  • Reputation Engine: Composite 0–10,000 bps score tracking dispute outcomes and falsifiable claim accuracy.
  • Run Simulation: Walks the exact contract call sequence locally so anyone can see the mechanism work without a funded wallet.
